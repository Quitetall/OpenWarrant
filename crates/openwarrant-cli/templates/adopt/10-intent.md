# Intent

Adopt OpenWarrant in {{program}}: bring the program's first unit of work from
`war new` to a human-signed resolution, with the SAS accepted and pinned, an
independent verification on record, and gate evidence minted — so that every
later Warrant follows a path this one has already walked.

## Out of scope

Everything else in {{program}}. Features, fixes and every other change go
ahead without this Warrant and without waiting for it; this one covers only
the adoption steps above.
