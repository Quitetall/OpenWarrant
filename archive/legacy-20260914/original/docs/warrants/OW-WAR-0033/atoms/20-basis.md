# Basis

## Governing Sources

SAS §17.5 (read projections), §103 (default full Warrant rendering), Appendix B (Work Order projection shape).

## Prerequisites

OW-WAR-0016, OW-WAR-0022, OW-WAR-0023 resolved — assurance, resolution, and dispatch must exist before they can be projected.

## Assumptions and Unknowns

- **Evidenced premise.** Projections are pure functions of the IR and the two existing ones prove the pattern.
- **Accepted residual risk.** A projection can omit something a reader needed, and omission is invisible.

## Constraints and Invariants

- **A projection is never a second source** (§22.4). Editing one is refused exactly as for the parent.
- **Inapplicable sections are omitted, not rendered empty** (§16.1).
- **No projection changes the contract digest** (§91.1 test 3).
