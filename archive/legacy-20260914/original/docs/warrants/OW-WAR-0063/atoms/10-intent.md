# Intent

Close the gap between "the control exists" and "the control has been seen to
refuse", for the obligations an independent verifier named.

## Where it stood

Two rounds of blind verification left sixty-three obligations unestablished.
Read one by one, most of them say the same thing: the code is there, and no
plant or test exercises the specific control the obligation names. §39.3
says an executed attack is evidence and a described one is a plan; the
verifier applied that rule and was right to.

## What this delivers

For the controls that exist, the missing executed attacks: a plant that
names a §106 row that does not exist (and the check that refuses it, which
did not exist either); a plant with a duplicate milestone id; a plant with a
contribution left unstated; a plant that edits the Markdown projection by
hand; and tests — an amendment leaves the prior revision's digest where an
attempt cited it; a provenance banner precedes the title and the renderer
divides nothing; every committed `WAR.json` carries the SAS pin; a
diagnostic line has §71.7's shape; allocation preserves the UUID; an ADR's
supersession relations are read in both directions, which needed two fields
the record did not have.

And two artifact-shaped gaps: the §94 baseline is re-taken at a named
commit and declared as a deliverable, so the verifier can read it rather
than be told about it.

## What this does not do

It does not manufacture evidence for work that has not happened. Katana
(0026, 0045), Liminal (0040, 0048), the Knowledge Fabric (0028, 0029, 0044),
a real agent over the §75.2 seam (0042), the schema pack (0032), and the
commit `3678455` that the ADR-overview detector was to find — which no
longer exists in this history after squash merges — stay unestablished with
their reasons on record.
