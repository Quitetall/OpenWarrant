# Basis

## Governing Sources

SAS §66: purpose, authority, event envelope, material events.

## Prerequisites

OW-WAR-0008 resolved.

## Assumptions and Unknowns

- **Evidenced premise.** §66 specifies the envelope and the material event list.
- **Accepted residual risk.** A local journal can be edited. It is provenance, not proof, and §66.2 says so.

## Constraints and Invariants

- **Append-only.** Rewriting history is the failure the journal exists to prevent.
- **Not authoritative** (§66.2). A journal entry never outranks a KF record.
- **Material events only** (§66.4). Journalling everything makes it unreadable.
