# Intent

## Problem

Nothing can close a Warrant. There is no resolution object, so `war check`'s
verdict is about form and the Overview says outright that nothing there means
done.

§56 requires a resolution to bind the EXACT contract and assurance snapshot
(RQ-059), and §56.3 makes falsification a valid resolution rather than a failure —
a disproved claim closes with the measurement that disproved it.

## Desired Outcome

A Warrant resolves against an exact contract revision and assurance snapshot. Falsification is a first-class outcome. Dispute preserves the original resolution; annulment changes standing without erasing history.

## Scope

Resolution requirements and records (§56.1–§56.2), falsification (§56.3), dispute (§56.4), annulment (§56.5), supersession (§56.6), and ongoing validation (§57).

## Non-goals

- No KF-side resolution authority; that is OW-WAR-0028.
