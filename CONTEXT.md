# OpenWarrant context

OpenWarrant owns a document standard and SDK. Providers such as LAMU compile
context; apps and harnesses run workflows. Canonical candidate definitions:
[RC.3 SAS, context unit](docs/sas/drafts/1.0.0-rc.3/WAR_Software_Architecture_Specification.md).
The candidate is not signed adoption or proof of shipped behavior.

- **Warrant:** one reviewable outcome, with scope, context, constraints and checks.
- **Completion:** the declared work is done; it may remain unverified.
- **Verified:** the exact result meets the baseline, independent evidence and
  secure human acceptance requirements. A performer cannot award this mark.
- **Action gate:** an explicit requirement before a named action. Qualification-only
  conditions do not impose universal approval before prompt-only work.
- **Source unit:** an exact marked text span in a versioned source document.
- **Projection:** selected context derived from sources; it does not grant authority.
- **Work stop:** declared scope complete, with generated progress and evidence links.
- **Agent/harness stop:** interrupted execution, not completion.

For current legacy CLI records and their exact historical terms, read
[legacy context](archive/legacy-20260914/original/CONTEXT.md). The legacy authorization/resolution
model still applies to its signed records; new words do not migrate those records.
