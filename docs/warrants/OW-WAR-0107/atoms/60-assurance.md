# Assurance

### OBL-001 — independent verification binds exact candidate
- **scope:** configured synthetic processes, real HTTP and separate Git workspaces.
- **evidence:** successful isolated verifier with exact source/revision/checks;
  self-verifier, mutable/stale candidate, missing protection and malformed result refuse.

### OBL-002 — repair and rebuttal loops preserve gates and limits
- **scope:** configured reference workflow and retained records.
- **evidence:** failed check triggers bounded repair and new verification; exhaustion
  stops; rebuttal cannot clear its own gate; unresolved disagreement waits for human;
  time/cost/repair bounds and restart/idempotency have observed refusal cases.

### OBL-003 — UI and reports remain truthful
- **scope:** reference API/browser and deterministic progress projections.
- **evidence:** browser shows findings, repairs and escalation; unknown differs from
  fail; prototype completion remains unverified; no fabricated mark; full gate passes.
