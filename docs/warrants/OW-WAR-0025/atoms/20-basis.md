# Basis

## Governing Sources

SAS §53: blocker, deviation, decision proposal, discovered gap, different remedies.

## Prerequisites

OW-WAR-0008 and OW-WAR-0024 resolved.

## Assumptions and Unknowns

- **Evidenced premise.** §53 enumerates all four with their remedies.
- **Accepted residual risk.** Choosing which of the four a situation is remains a
  judgement.

## Constraints and Invariants

- **Blocking does not erase phase** (§91.6 test 37), already required by the
  state model.
- **Four remedies, four records** (§53.5). One 'issue' type would lose the routing.
- **A discovered gap is not a deviation** — one says the plan was wrong, the other
  says the execution differed.
