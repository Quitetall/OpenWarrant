# Intent

## Problem

When execution goes wrong there is nowhere to say so. §53 gives four distinct
records with four distinct remedies (§53.5): a blocker stops work, a deviation
records doing it differently, a decision proposal escalates a normative choice,
and a discovered gap says the plan was incomplete. Collapsing them into 'a
problem' loses the remedy.

## Desired Outcome

Execution problems are typed records with distinct remedies. A blocker blocks without erasing phase; a discovered gap surfaces as work the plan did not contain.

## Scope

The four record types (§53.1–§53.4) and their distinct remedies (§53.5).

## Non-goals

- No automatic remediation. These records route a problem; a human or a new Warrant resolves it.

## SAS and Roadmap Traceability

No §106 requirement maps here directly; enabling work named in the Production Roadmap.
