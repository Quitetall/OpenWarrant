# Assurance

### OBL-001 — Three real consenting participants
- **scope:** P1,P2,P3 sessions using exact pinned workflow builds and disposable repositories.
- **evidence:** Redacted consent confirmation and observed session records; missing consent or synthetic participants refuse qualification.

### OBL-002 — Low-friction thresholds measured
- **scope:** Every participant, not an average: setup ≤600 seconds and administration ≤60 seconds.
- **evidence:** Reproducible totals from classified event intervals; missing measurements, assistance and over-threshold cases remain visible and cannot pass silently.

### OBL-003 — Real completion and retained failures
- **scope:** One declared Warrant outcome per participant.
- **evidence:** Exact result/evidence pointers, observed completion and independent review of the study; interrupted or failed attempts are retained and do not become completed sessions.
