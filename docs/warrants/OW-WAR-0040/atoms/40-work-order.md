# Work Order

## Deliverables

1. The Liminal profile for WAR documents.
2. The Liminal source adapter.
3. A parity harness comparing both adapters' canonical IR over the whole corpus.
4. A cutover criterion stated as a number, not a judgement.

## Frozen Surfaces

The canonical IR. Two adapters producing it means the IR is the contract, and it must not bend to suit either.

## Premade Instructions

- Do not start until Liminal is checked out. A guess at another system's
  semantics is worse here than anywhere else, because the output is the
  canonical form.
- Parity over the WHOLE corpus, not a sample. A sample leaves the untested
  document as the one that differs.

## Autonomy and Escalation

Tier T1.

## Rollback

Revert. The Markdown adapter remains the only one, which is a supported state — §20 of the resolved decisions ships it first on purpose.
