# Intent

Replace the false export round-trip claim with a content-bearing preservation path.
A user can export retained Warrant records, move the archive away from its source,
import into an empty compatible destination, and re-export identical canonical
bytes while preserving historical states and evidence identities.

This follows OW30's unfulfilled preservation outcome without rewriting its signed
history. Out of scope: granting imported execution authority, inventing missing
history or evidence, deleting source records, or redefining old export digests.
