# Intent

Generate deterministic work-stop reports from retained reference-workflow execution
records. Users get a short response, progress pointer and downloadable offline HTML.
Completion remains distinct from qualification. This closes the report surface;
hotline routing, fencing/recovery and real-user qualification remain separate work.
