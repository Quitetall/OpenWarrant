# Intent

## Problem

§40.7 forbids a performer's report from becoming independent evidence. §46 says
what independence actually MEANS — its dimensions, what a blind verifier is
given, and the minimums per assurance level. Without it, 'independent' is a word
anyone can apply to themselves.

## Desired Outcome

Independence is a typed property with dimensions and declared minimums. A verifier that fails the minimum for an assurance level cannot satisfy its obligations. Blind verifier input is constructed rather than assumed.

## Scope

Independence dimensions (§46.1), blind verifier input (§46.2), and minimums by assurance level (§46.3).

## Non-goals

- No human workflow enforcement; independent human review is Phase 9 (release).
