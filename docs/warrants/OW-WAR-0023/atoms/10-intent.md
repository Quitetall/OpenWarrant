# Intent

## Problem

A stage cannot be handed to anyone. §47 defines a Dispatch as everything ONE
actor needs — exact basis, capabilities, resources, outputs, stop conditions — and
§47.3 requires an actor-specific projection so an actor receives only what its
role requires, not the whole Warrant.

## Desired Outcome

A stage compiles to a Dispatch carrying its exact basis and bounded authority. A stateless actor can execute from the Dispatch alone.

## Scope

The Dispatch schema (§47.1), compilation (§47.2), and actor-specific projection (§47.3).

## Non-goals

- No runtime. Executing a Dispatch is OW-WAR-0026 (Katana) and OW-WAR-0027 (BLUT).
