# Basis

## Governing Sources

SAS §40 (epistemic classes, prohibited substitutions), §41 (evidence model, admissibility, chain of custody), §42 (judgment model).

## Prerequisites

OW-WAR-0016 resolved — obligations are what evidence is offered against.

## Assumptions and Unknowns

- **Evidenced premise.** §40.7 enumerates the prohibited substitutions
  explicitly, so this is enforcement of a stated list.
- **Accepted residual risk.** Admissibility (§41.2) is partly a judgement about
  source independence, which a type system can structure but not decide.

## Constraints and Invariants

- **A performer report is never independent evidence** (§40.7, RQ-053). This is
  the single most important rule in the system.
- **Inference connects observation to claim** and cannot be substituted for
  either.
- **Judgment is accountable** (§42) — it names who judged.
- **Unknown is not failure and not pass** (Law 15), already honoured by the
  diagnostic model and now extended to evidence.
