# Intent

Replace prompt-only authority-file protection with inert proposals and authenticated
transitions. Agents can prepare changes without gaining their proposed roles.
Deliver a pure SDK, reference CLI/OpenSSH adapter, protected-store contract,
retained-history migration, and a Linux execution sandbox example.

Out of scope: signing as the owner, accepting SAS RC.3, changing effective authority
in this repository, replacing historical signature subjects, or claiming production
qualification without deployment evidence. Existing legacy record consumers remain
legacy until an explicit trusted workflow cutover; they must not be described as
protected by installing this candidate alone.
