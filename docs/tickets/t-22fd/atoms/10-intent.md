# Signing must not make evidence stale

Under OW-WAR-0133 a receipt from a gate with no declared inputs binds the whole tree, and authorization/response/attestation records are not excluded — so a human signature recorded after evidence stales every receipt, forcing two separate human sittings (authorize, then resolve). Decide whether authority records belong in the source Exclusions, or whether gates should declare inputs, and make one sitting possible.

- **2026-09-25 22:20 UTC, claude:** **Decision (i-3499): both, with the exclusion doing the work.** The tree fallback (gates with no `inputs`) now skips the records a human act writes, named file by file (`source::is_authority_record`, gate_cmd.rs): responses, batches, per-Warrant authorization.toml / authorization.<8hex>.toml / judgments.toml / resolution.toml / attestations/ / corrections/ / disputes/ / questions/, gates/invalidations/, standing/attestations/, and the SAS and roadmap revisions/attestations/. Declared `inputs` are NOT narrowed: a gate that declares it reads an authority record is held to it.
  
  **Why not declare inputs instead.** The gates this corpus cites — software.repo.war-check and the battery — read the whole corpus including authority records (a signature can change war check's result). Declaring that as inputs binds them to every signature: the two sittings come back. What such a receipt would say about authority records is established elsewhere, more strictly: each act's ingest verifies its record (signature over exact bytes, bound digest, role); a resolution re-judges live the records it rests on (req 1 re-verifies the authorization signature for the exact contract; 9 judgments; 11 risk acceptances; 13 resolver role); an invalidation stops receipts by name (evidence.gate-invalidated). Stated limit: a receipt of a gate that reads authority records says nothing about authority records written after it; `war check` run now still reads them all.
  
  **Not weakened.** Any other moved path still stales by name: source, atoms, deliverables, Gate Definitions, verifications, and the trust roots roles.toml / allowed_signers (hand-edited, not the record of an act). SAS and roadmap revision records stay bound: accepting one changes the specification the corpus is judged against — it is source, and still stales (so it goes before the evidence). Recorded in docs/RESOLVING.md (reuse rule, 'A signature does not move the tree') and docs/SIGNING.md ('One sitting').
- **2026-09-25 22:27 UTC, claude:** **One sitting (i-71d8).** A single `war sign --batch` cannot carry authorize and resolve for the same Warrant, by three deliberate rules: a resolution is pending only for an authorized revision (sign.rs `pending`); its §56.1 requirements are judged when drafted, requirement 1 being that authorization; and a batch is one signer in one role (authorizer vs resolver). Changing the one-role batch is a change to the authority design, not in reach of this ticket. So the sitting is two batches with no agent step between: (SAS/roadmap acceptance first and alone, only if one governs — it stales evidence by design), then `war sign --batch --ssh-sign` (authorizations), then `war sign --batch --ssh-sign` again (resolutions), then commit. The agent's last act before the sitting is `war evidence record` + commit (a verification committed after evidence moves the tree). Written in docs/SIGNING.md 'One sitting'.
