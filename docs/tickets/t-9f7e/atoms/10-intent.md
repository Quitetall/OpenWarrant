# Verification bundles too large for the blind verifier

OW-WAR-0112's bundle is 241,152 tokens; the blind verifier (tools/verifier/claude-verifier.sh, 900 s timeout) timed out on it (2026-09-26). Large Warrants cannot be verified at all. Options: per-obligation bundles carrying only what that obligation's evidence and scope name (its deliverables, its plants, its gate runs); bounded excerpts with digests of the whole; or a verifier that walks obligations one call at a time. Keep the blindness guarantees (no performer narrative, no tools).

- **2026-09-26 15:38 UTC, claude:** Design (i-a20b). Chosen: a reading budget per bundle, [verify] max_bundle_tokens (default 48000, estimated bytes/4 over the WHOLE canonical JSON, not just atoms+deliverables as before). A Warrant whose bundle fits stays ONE warrant-scope bundle exactly as before (one verifier call; OW-WAR-0146's claims about tail text and one-file-after-two-runs still hold). Over budget, it becomes ONE BUNDLE PER OBLIGATION: the deliverables whose obligation_refs list it (deliverables.toml already declares these for every wave Warrant) plus any its statement/scope/evidence names by path; the rest listed by id+sha256 under deliverables_not_carried. The byte allowance left after the fixed parts (atoms, request, records) is water-filled over the carried files and gate streams; a file over its share is carried as numbered-line excerpts - its head (<=30 lines, <=1/4 of its share), then windows around each line naming one of the obligation's backticked terms, round-robin across terms - with the whole file's sha256, bytes, lines and truncated:true. Gate streams keep the tail as text, plus the '== ... (alias) ==' sections, term lines and FAIL/ERROR lines. obligation_evidence says, per obligation, each named path carried or absent-and-why, and each named term shown_in / cut_from - so absent or cut evidence is said, never dropped. Blind: same sources as before, no new field for performer text; the verifier script keeps --tools '' etc. and its flags unchanged. Deterministic: every step is a function of tree+config (verifications/ is created before building so a first run describes the same tree as the second). --run calls the verifier once per bundle, each under verifier_timeout_secs; a response answering an obligation its bundle did not carry is refused (verify.outside-bundle); a failed/timed-out call records nothing for its obligations and the rest still ingest. Why not excerpts alone: 0112's 44 files x even 4 KiB each plus atoms still leaves ~1 KiB per file for 7 obligations - per-obligation selection is what makes room for the lines that matter. Why not always split: small Warrants would pay N calls for nothing.
